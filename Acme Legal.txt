SERVICE AGREEMENT

THIS SERVICE AGREEMENT ("Agreement") is entered into as of 10 June 2026, by and between Acme Legal Services (Pty) Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of South Africa ("Provider"), and the counterparty identified in Schedule A ("Client").

WHEREAS the Provider is engaged in the business of supplying legal document processing services; and WHEREAS the Client wishes to engage the Provider on the terms set out herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
1.2 "Services" means the document intake, storage, and processing services described in Schedule B.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Provider shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with applicable laws of the jurisdiction of South Africa and the timelines set out in Schedule B.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the fees set out in Schedule C within thirty (30) days of the date of each valid invoice. Late payments shall bear interest at the prescribed rate.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice. Obligations of confidentiality shall survive termination.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
